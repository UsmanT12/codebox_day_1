# Trace demo script

About 80 seconds at a steady pace, including brief pauses for the demo.

## 0–20 seconds — Slide 1: Tech stack

Trace is a nutrition tracker built with React and Vite on the frontend, and Node.js with Express on the backend. Supabase handles user accounts and stores food entries. USDA FoodData Central provides the nutrition information.

## 20–40 seconds — Slide 2, then switch to the app

I built Trace to make it easier to understand what I eat, especially micronutrients like iron, calcium, and vitamin D. After signing in, each user has their own diary. I can search for eggs and enter a quantity in grams, ounces, or servings.

## 40–60 seconds — Add a food and show the dashboard

When I add a food, the dashboard updates my calories, macros, and micronutrients. The bars compare my intake with my daily goals. For micronutrients, anything at ninety percent or higher appears green.

## 60–80 seconds — Show Daily goals, then Weekly

I can change my daily goals and save them to my account. The weekly view shows my average intake across seven days. My goal was a simple app that makes food logging and nutrient tracking easy.
